Enterprise-Bench Contribution

Task-design feedback, a five-task pilot, and agent failure analysis.

I contributed task-design feedback and evaluation analysis to DevRev’s open Enterprise-Bench. My contribution involved reviewing L2 tasks, piloting five UK L2 tasks, and discussing findings with the team during an official livestream.

The pilot

I ran five tasks with GPT-5.6 Luna; four passed in that single run. This is a small pilot observation, not a repeated evaluation or an overall benchmark score.

Looking beyond the pass count

I examined a late-email failure and proposed virtual-clock checks for time-dependent tasks. I also suggested sandboxing safeguards and clearer evaluation visualizations to make multi-step behavior and failures easier to inspect.

My contribution

The work was task-design feedback, pilot evaluation, and failure analysis. Enterprise-Bench is DevRev’s benchmark; I do not claim authorship of the full benchmark or its evaluation infrastructure.
