Legal-AI Evaluation Data

A reproducible benchmark data pipeline built at CIPS-AI Labs.

As a Post-Training Researcher at CIPS-AI Labs, I built a reproducible, validation-checked pipeline for legal-AI evaluation across 873 Indian Supreme Court land-dispute cases.

Making the data evaluable

I organized the data into 12 evaluation views spanning eight judgment sections and four precedent-sentiment categories. The work connects benchmark curation with explicit coverage and processing checks.

Reported processing outcome

  • 4.19M+ words generated across 790 content-bearing records.
  • 100% case-ID coverage and zero processing errors in the reported run.
  • 83 legitimately unsegmented cases tracked separately as a data-quality outcome.

Case-ID coverage does not mean every case produced segmented content. Keeping those two counts separate makes the benchmark’s coverage easier to interpret.

Reliability during generation

I used a five-case smoke test, resumable checkpoints, validation gates, and recovery steps for long-running jobs. These checks make incomplete or interrupted processing visible before it becomes downstream evaluation data.

Scope and limitations

These figures describe dataset processing and curation. They are not model-accuracy results, and zero processing errors does not establish that every generated record is semantically correct. This page summarizes my work; it does not publish the underlying workplace corpus.
