Thadam

In development

Every agent leaves a trace. How do we know it behaved correctly?

I’m working on open-source testing, evaluation, and reliability tooling for AI agents. Thadam runs developer-defined checks, captures execution evidence, and compares changes against reviewed baselines.

Current capabilities include tool-use assertions, saved-report comparisons, and repeated-run reliability gates. Scenario generation and a dashboard are planned.
